## Required
- [ ] Alignment statements included (Safety / Competence / Belonging)

- [ ] Metrics impact defined (which dashboards move and why)

## Safety (Dignity)
- [ ] Corrections are collaborative, not judgmental
- [ ] Clear recovery path after failure

## Competence (Growth)
- [ ] Produces an observable growth signal (skill delta, time-to-success, difficulty tolerance)

## Belonging (Continuity)
- [ ] Adds/maintains continuity (resume state, narrative thread, memory)
