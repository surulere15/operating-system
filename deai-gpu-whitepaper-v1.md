## Three Structural Shifts Driving This

### 1. The "Model Collapse" Arbitrage

Open-source models (Llama 4, Mistral V5) now outperform closed-source models in narrow vertical domains:

- **Legal:** Contract review accuracy matching GPT-4 at 1/20th the cost
- **Medical:** Diagnostic assistance outperforming proprietary models in specific pathology categories
- **Logistics:** Route optimization models trained on proprietary fleet data, deployed at edge

**Implication:** You no longer need OpenAI/Anthropic pricing to production-grade AI. Specialized open models + cheap decentralized compute = viable AI startups at fraction of traditional cost.

### 2. Node Provenance & Ethical Sourcing

Buyers now demand proof of geographic and ethical origin for training data and compute. This is the "Phase 8 alignment" problem:

- Where was this GPU manufactured?
- What jurisdiction runs this node?
- Is training data consent-verified?

DeAI protocols are building **provenance attestation layers** that centralized providers cannot replicate because they don't control the supply chain.

### 3. Federated Fine-Tuning & Inference at the Edge

The next wave isn't about where you train — it's about training *without data leaving the device*:

- Federated learning protocols that train across private edge devices
- Inference-as-a-Service moving from centralized to edge nodes
- Data never leaves the sovereign boundary of the data owner

This isn't theoretical. Protocols are live. Companies are deploying.

The remaining gap is closing monthly.
