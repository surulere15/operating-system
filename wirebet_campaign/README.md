# Wirebet vs BidAndBet Viral Campaign

## Objective
Validate brand preference at scale
Generate engagement + attention
Create momentum you can reuse (content + audience)

## 48-HOUR SPRINT

### PHASE 1 — SETUP (Hours 0–4)
1. Lock 3 High-Impact Hooks (no more than 3)
   - Hook A (Conflict): "One of these betting brands could go global… the other won’t survive."
   - Hook B (Ego): "If you had $5M to build a betting company, which name are you choosing?"
   - Hook C (Controversy): "Most people will pick the wrong name here."

2. Create 3 Visuals (keep it simple, bold)
   Each visual:
   - Big hook (top)
   - Wirebet ⚡️ vs BidAndBet 🎯 (center)
   - "Vote now" (bottom)
   Format: 1080×1920 (TikTok/Reels) and 1080×1080 (X/IG fallback)

3. Prepare Comment Triggers (VERY IMPORTANT)
   Pre-write 6–10 replies:
   - "You picked wrong 😅 explain your logic"
   - "Interesting… founders usually choose the other one"
   - "This is exactly why branding matters"
   - "You’re thinking short-term, not scale"
   - "Respect. But would this compete with Bet365?"

### PHASE 2 — LAUNCH (Hours 4–12)
1. TikTok (Primary engine)
   Post 2 videos back-to-back (different hooks)
   Format: 7–12 seconds, fast text animation, light background music
   Caption: "Be honest. Which one wins? 👇 #Wirebet #BidAndBet #StartupBrand #Betting"

2. X (Twitter) Poll (Critical)
   Post: "Which name has the strongest potential to compete globally?"
   Options: Wirebet.com, BidAndBet.com
   Follow-up tweet: "Most people will get this wrong."

3. Instagram
   Carousel (same visuals)
   Story with poll sticker

4. LinkedIn (Optional but valuable)
   Frame as: branding + investment decision

### PHASE 3 — IGNITION (Hours 12–24)
Immediately after posting:
1. Reply to EVERY comment using pre-written triggers
2. Seed conversations manually (reply to own post, tag small creators)
3. Push visibility loops (like every comment, pin controversial comment, repost own tweet)

### PHASE 4 — DOUBLE DOWN (Hours 24–36)
Identify winning hook/platform, create 2nd Wave Content:
1. "Results" Post: "Results after 24 hours… and it’s not what I expected"
2. "People are choosing wrong" Post: "Here’s why most people are picking the weaker brand"
3. "Expert vs Crowd" Angle: "Founders choose differently than the public"
Post again on TikTok + X

### PHASE 5 — EXPANSION (Hours 36–48)
1. Enter Communities (Reddit, niche conversations)
2. Micro-Influencer Trigger (reply to betting/branding tweets with quick question)
3. Optional Funnel (if traction exists): add simple link to waitlist/early supporters club

## SUCCESS METRICS (after 48 hours)
- Comments > Likes? ✅ good
- People arguing? ✅ very good
- Shares happening? ✅ strong signal
- Clear winner? ✅ validation

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGY
- X (Twitter): Debate engine, investor visibility
- LinkedIn: Credibility layer, seriousness
- TikTok: Attention engine, viral reach
- Reddit: Truth filter, raw opinions
