Recent reports indicate a surge in roofing damage due to frequent storm events across the US. Roofing companies are experiencing increased calls related to storm damage, including wind, hail, and ice dams. Be aware that some roofing companies might try to get homeowners to sign contingency contracts prior to inspection, which could be disguised as inspection reports. Homeowners should document all damage with photos and videos and provide them to their insurance adjuster, along with a detailed inspection report.
